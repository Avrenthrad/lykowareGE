# ADR 0098 — The sky behind the scene

Status: Proposed.
Related task/PR: `07.05d`, the fourth slice of `07.05`, branch `claude/07.05d-sky`.
Supersedes / superseded by: none. Builds on ADR 0082 (the stated light), ADR 0086 (per-draw pipelines), ADR 0087 (the HDR target) and ADR 0026 (the first renderer).

## Context

- **What exists:** behind the scene is one clear colour, passed to `draw()`. Outdoors that is a flat wall of colour with no horizon and no sun.
- **Constraints:**
  - The sky must sit behind everything without a second pass and without changing how the scene draws.
  - The sun must be where the scene's light comes from, or shading and sky disagree.
  - With the sky off, images must not change, byte for byte.
  - Distance fog was planned for this slice. It needs the distance in every scene shader, and the lit block is full at 128 bytes (ADR 0085, ADR 0086), so it is a change to every pipeline. It is split out as `07.05e`.

## Options

- **A sky box or dome mesh.** Works with today's draws, but needs a texture or a large mesh and care at the far plane.
- **One triangle over the whole image, coloured from each pixel's view direction.** No assets, exact, and drawn first with depth writes off so the scene covers it.
- **An atmosphere model (scattering).** The right look for time of day. Much more shader, and nothing yet sets a time of day.

## Decision

- **A full-image draw, first in the scene's pass.** `Renderer::enable_sky(SkyShaders, Sky)` builds one pipeline from `engine/shaders/sky.slang`: depth tested and not written, no blending, a 112-byte block. Each `draw()` then puts one triangle past the screen's corners at the front of the pass. Depth is reversed and cleared to 0 (infinitely far), so the triangle passes at any depth above 0, writes none, and every scene draw covers it.
- **The formula.** For a pixel whose unit view direction in world space is d:
  - inside the sun's disc, where `d · s ≥ cos(sun_radius)` with s the unit `Lighting::direction`: `Sky::sun`;
  - otherwise `mix(horizon, zenith, max(d.y, 0))`. At and below the horizon that is `horizon`.
  - d is `forward + ndc.x × right × tan(fov_x / 2) − ndc.y × up × tan(fov_y / 2)`, normalised, from the camera's own axes. It does not depend on where the camera is, so it is as exact far from the origin as near it (ADR 0021).
- **The sun follows the light.** The direction is read from the renderer's lighting at each draw, not stored in `Sky`. `sun_radius` 0 means no sun.
- **Who owns what.** The renderer owns the pipeline, its HDR copy and the triangle (shared with the post pass). `disable_sky()` keeps the pipeline alive, because a swapchain frame may still use it; it is freed with the renderer or replaced by the next enable.
- **Counting.** The sky is not an entity: it is left out of `last_draw_count()` and `DrawStats`, so batching numbers mean the same with or without it.
- **Failure.** `enable_sky` returns false and leaves the sky as it was for a skinned renderer (`InvalidArgument`), a colour channel below 0 or not finite, a `sun_radius` outside 0..`sky_max_sun_radius` or not finite (`InvalidArgument`), or shaders that do not load or link. Without the shaders there is no sky and the clear colour shows. That is what a missing shader does.

## Contracts and compatibility

- New: `render::Sky`, `render::SkyShaders`, `sky_max_sun_radius`, `Renderer::enable_sky`, `disable_sky`, `sky`; `engine/shaders/sky.slang`.
- Defaults keep every existing draw as it was: the sky is off.
- With post-processing on (ADR 0087) the sky is drawn into the float target, so colours above 1 are kept and tone-mapped with the scene.
- Threading: the Device's thread, as the renderer today.
- Scene files do not store a sky yet. That is GPT's format.

## Consequences

- Outdoor scenes have a horizon and a sun that agrees with the shading. A water surface's `reflection` (ADR 0097) can be set from the same colours.
- The sun's edge is hard, and the sky does not light the scene: ambient is still `Lighting::ambient`.
- Distance fog (`07.05e`), clouds, a moon and stars, time of day and scattering are later work.

## Verification

- `render.sky` (lavapipe locally; CI on the five hosted jobs): with no entities, every eighth pixel of a level view matches the gradient for its own view direction within 2/255; so does a tilted view far from the origin. Below the horizon is the horizon colour. With the sky's draw removed the same check is off by more than 100. Looking at the light, the centre pixel is the sun's colour exactly; with `sun_radius` 0 or after the light moves, it is the gradient. A cube drawn under the sky keeps its exact colour, the sky shows around it, and the cube is the one counted draw. With post-processing on, the sky matches the Reinhard-mapped gradient and a sun of 4 maps to 0.8. Disabling the sky gives the earlier image byte for byte. Refused: a NaN or negative colour, a `sun_radius` of 0.6, missing shaders, a skinned renderer.
