# ADR 0024 — The Lykoware maths module

Status: Proposed. The owner chose "write our own" on 2026-11-04; the conventions below wait for the owner.
Related task/PR: 3D rendering, PR 1 (branch `claude/math`).
Supersedes / superseded by: none. Replaces the temporary `Vec3`, `DVec3` and `Quat` in the physics API, as [ADR 0020](0020-physics-api.md) planned.

## Context

3D rendering needs vectors, rotations, 4x4 matrices and camera maths. The physics API carried minimal vector types "until the engine has a maths module". Positions are double precision ([ADR 0022](0022-double-precision-positions.md)), but GPUs work in single precision, so world positions must reach the GPU relative to the camera ([ADR 0021](0021-product-targets.md)).

## Options

The owner was asked on 2026-11-04:

- **Write our own** (chosen). Small, fully ours, and we control which operations round identically on every platform.
- **GLM** (MIT). Complete and widely used, but large, and its exact rounding across compilers is harder to guarantee.

## Decision

Proposed:

- **`lykoware/math.hpp`**, namespace `lykoware::math`, header only:
  - `Vec2`, `Vec3`, `Vec4` (`float`), `DVec3` (`double`), `Quat` (unit quaternion, default no rotation) and `Mat4` (default identity);
  - the usual operations: arithmetic, dot, cross, length, normalize, lerp, quaternion product, rotate and conjugate, matrix product, translation, scaling, rotation, `trs`, `rigid_inverse`, `look_at` and `perspective`.
- **Conventions:**
  - Right-handed, +Y up, metres and radians; a camera looks down its -Z axis.
  - Matrices are column-major (`m[column][row]`) and multiply column vectors, as Vulkan and Slang expect.
  - `a * b` for quaternions and matrices applies `b` first.
- **Projection:** Vulkan clip space (Y down, depth 0 to 1) with **reversed, infinite depth**. The near plane has depth 1, and depth falls towards 0 with distance without reaching it. Reversed depth spreads float precision evenly over distance, which a 16 km world needs.
- **Camera-relative positions:** `relative_to(position, origin)` subtracts in double and only then rounds to float. A sub-millimetre offset 16 km out survives; computed in float, it would be lost.
- **Safe edge cases:**
  - normalizing a zero vector gives zero, and a zero quaternion gives no rotation;
  - `from_axis_angle` with a zero axis gives no rotation;
  - `look_at` with the target at the eye, or looking straight along `up`, gives the identity.
- **Determinism:** `+`, `-`, `*`, `/` and `sqrt` round identically everywhere (IEEE 754). `from_axis_angle` and `perspective` call the C library's `sin`, `cos` and `tan`, which can differ in the last bit between platforms. They are for cameras and drawing, not for simulation state that must replay identically.
- **Physics:** `physics::Vec3`, `physics::DVec3` and `physics::Quat` are now aliases of the maths types. Same layout and field names, so physics code, its callers and every recorded physics hash are unchanged.

## Contracts and compatibility

- New public header `lykoware/math.hpp`. `lykoware/physics.hpp` includes it.
- Name clash found: a test's own `length(Vec3)` helper became ambiguous with `math::length`, found through argument-dependent lookup. The test now uses `math::length`. Code that defines its own functions on these types may hit the same; the fix is to use the maths module's.

## Consequences

- Rendering, physics and the ECS share one set of value types.
- Matrices are `float` only; double-precision transforms stay as `DVec3` positions plus `relative_to`.
- Not yet: `Mat3`, general matrix inverse, `slerp`, frustum and bounding-box helpers. Each comes with its caller.

## Verification

- **New test `math.types`** (links only the engine):
  - **Vectors:** the right-hand rule, dot, length, normalize (including zero), lerp and arithmetic.
  - **Camera-relative offsets:** 0.4 mm and 0.3 mm offsets 16 km out survive `relative_to`.
  - **Quaternions:**
    - a quarter turn about +Y takes +X to -Z;
    - two quarter turns make a half;
    - for general rotations, `a * b` means b, then a;
    - the conjugate undoes a rotation;
    - zero axes and zero quaternions give no rotation.
  - **Matrices:**
    - the rotation matrix agrees with the quaternion;
    - `trs` scales, then rotates, then translates;
    - products are associative;
    - `rigid_inverse` undoes a rigid transform.
  - **Camera:**
    - `look_at` puts the eye at the origin and the target down -Z, with world +X to the right and up as up;
    - degenerate inputs give the identity.
  - **Projection:**
    - the near plane has depth 1;
    - depth falls with distance and stays above 0 at 16 km;
    - view-space up is the top of the image;
    - the aspect ratio widens the view.
- **Deliberate breaks:**
  - **Caught first time:**
    - a flipped cross product;
    - non-reversed depth;
    - a sign error in the rotation matrix;
    - no Vulkan Y flip.
  - **Slipped through at first, now caught:**
    - `relative_to` subtracting in float: the first test values happened to be exact in float;
    - a quaternion product with two terms swapped: the first tests used special angles that hide it.

    Both tests were strengthened.
- **Physics:** every physics hash is unchanged with the aliases.
- **Local results:** 36/36 pass in the Debug, ASan/UBSan and clang-tidy presets.
- **CI:** #32 [run 41820018128](https://github.com/Avrenthrad/lykowareGE/actions/runs/41820018128) on dd00780 passed all five jobs (Windows MSVC Debug and Release included).
